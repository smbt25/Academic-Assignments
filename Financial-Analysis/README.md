# 📊 Nestlé S.A. — Financial Analysis & Investment Advisory Report

An institutional-grade financial analysis and investment advisory report evaluating **Nestlé S.A. (NESN.S)** for a **£2,000,000** institutional investment allocation. 

Completed as part of the **C31FN Financial Analysis** course at **Heriot-Watt University Business School**.

---

## 🚀 Executive Summary

This project provides a thorough evaluation of Nestlé's financial health, strategic positioning, operational efficiency, capital structure, ESG performance, and intrinsic valuation across a five-year review period (**2020–2024**). 

* **Investment Recommendation:** **BUY / INVEST (£2,000,000)**
* **Investor Profile:** Risk-averse, long-term institutional investors seeking stable dividend income and strong ESG compliance.
* **Key Thesis:** Despite short-term pricing-led demand pressures, margin compression, and recent leadership transitions, Nestlé remains a premier defensive FMCG leader. Its deliberate lean working capital strategy, robust operating cash flows, and proactive ESG execution cement its status as a stable value-oriented asset.

---

## 📈 Key Financial Highlights & Findings

* **Liquidity & Working Capital:** Maintained a Current Ratio below 1.0 ($0.82$ in 2024), reflecting a deliberate strategy to optimize working capital via extended trade payables and high inventory turnover rather than structural liquidity risk.
* **Leverage & Capital Structure:** The Debt-to-Equity ratio increased significantly from $0.86$ (2020) to $1.73$ (2024), driven by debt-funded share buybacks designed to boost Earnings Per Share (EPS) and optimize capital structure.
* **Profitability & Returns:** Gross Profit margins recovered to **16.02%** in 2024 following post-pandemic cost normalizations[cite: 10], while Return on Equity (ROE) remained exceptionally strong at **30.30%**.
* **Valuation:** Although slightly overvalued relative to certain baseline intrinsic models (Refinitiv consensus intrinsic value ~CHF 72.89 vs. market price ~CHF 74.88), Nestlé's consistent dividend yield and defensive characteristics justify its valuation for long-term holders.

---

## 🌱 ESG Performance Highlights

Nestlé has demonstrated strong leadership across environmental and social metrics:
* **Climate Action:** Achieved an absolute greenhouse gas emissions reduction of **20.38%**, surpassing its 2030 milestone ahead of schedule.
* **Circular Economy:** Reduced virgin plastic usage by **21.3%** against the 2018 baseline, with **86.4%** of plastic packaging designed for recycling.
* **Deforestation-Free Supply Chain:** Assessed **93.5%** of primary key commodities (cocoa, coffee, palm oil) as deforestation-free.

### **Conclusion**
Nestlé S.A. is established as an A-rated defensive stock that successfully combines long-term value creation, consistent dividend payouts, and robust ESG integration. While navigating a challenging macroeconomic environment marked by post-pandemic consumer shifts and inflation, Nestlé's deliberate working capital structure, operational efficiency, and global brand presence secure its market leadership. 

### **Practical Implications**
* **For Institutional Capital Allocation:** The proposed £2,000,000 investment is well-suited for risk-averse portfolios targeting stable, long-term recurring income and high sustainability standards.
* **Risk Monitoring & Financial Leverage:** Investors must carefully monitor rising debt-to-equity ratios resulting from aggressive share buybacks, as increased interest obligations can limit financial flexibility during adverse market scenarios.
* **External Cost & Competition Factors:** Management of raw material volatility (such as coffee, cocoa, and milk prices) and competitive pressures from private-label alternatives remain vital for safeguarding operating margins.
