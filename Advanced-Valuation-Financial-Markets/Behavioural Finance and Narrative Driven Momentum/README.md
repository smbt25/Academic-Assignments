# Behavioural Finance, Efficient Market Hypothesis, and Narrative-Driven Momentum: An Empirical Analysis of ARM Holdings and Eternal Limited

## Executive Summary
This report investigates the limitations of the Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH) through the lens of **Behavioural Finance** and **Prospect Theory**. By examining two high-profile, highly liquid equities—**ARM Holdings** (US market) and **Eternal Limited / Zomato** (Indian market)—the study analyzes how cognitive biases, narrative framing, loss aversion, and institutional herding drive extreme valuations detached from fundamental cash flows.

---

## Core Findings & Empirical Analysis

### 1. Theoretical Framework: EMH vs. Prospect Theory
* **Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH):** Fama’s (1970) EMH posits that share prices instantaneously reflect all available information, rendering excess market returns impossible through fundamental analysis.
* **Prospect Theory Value Function:** Developed by Kahneman and Tversky (1979), this framework demonstrates that individuals evaluate outcomes relative to a reference point rather than absolute wealth. Key deviations from rational behavior include:
  * **Loss Aversion:** Losses are felt approximately twice as intensely as equivalent gains.
  * **Diminishing Sensitivity & Reference Points:** Psychological impact diminishes for larger outcomes, leading to the **disposition effect** (selling winners too early and holding losers too long).
  * **Skewness Preference & Probability Weighting:** Investors overweight extreme tail scenarios (lottery-like payoffs), fueling speculative bubbles.

### 2. ARM Holdings (Nasdaq: ARM): Post-IPO AI Momentum
* **Price Performance:** Listed on NASDAQ on September 14, 2023, at $56.10 and surged to a peak of $186.46 by July 10, 2024 (a 266% return in under 10 months).
* **Catalyst & Narrative:** February 2024 earnings heavily emphasized an AI infrastructure narrative, presenting ARM's licensing model as an indispensable foundation for every AI device globally. The stock jumped 48% in a single session and over 93% across three sessions.
* **Valuation Disconnect:** Morningstar’s fair value estimate of $34 stood in stark contrast to its peak price, indicating a ~448% overvaluation driven by narrative momentum rather than independent rational analysis.
* **Behavioural Mechanisms:** 
  * *Underreaction & Overreaction:* Initial underreaction to pre-AI fundamentals transitioned into aggressive institutional herding driven by career risk among portfolio managers.
  * *Endowment Effect & Mental Accounting:* Early investors treated gains as "house money," increasing risk-taking appetite and fueling further buying pressure despite extreme multiples.

### 3. Eternal Limited (formerly Zomato Limited) (NSE: ETERNAL.NS): Profitability Narrative Momentum
* **Price Performance:** Listed on July 23, 2021, at INR 125.85, Zomato spent 18 months below its IPO price due to substantial historical losses (INR 12,230M in FY2022 and INR 9,710M in FY2023), placing long-term holders firmly in the loss domain of Prospect Theory.
* **Catalyst & Narrative:** Sentiment shifted dramatically when the company posted its first quarterly profit in mid-2023 and subsequent annual profits (INR 3,510M in FY2024; INR 5,270M in FY2025). Management framed this as proof of structural profitability for quick-commerce brand Blinkit within India's expanding digital economy.
* **Valuation Disconnect:** The stock soared 508% from INR 60.65 on January 1, 2023, to an all-time high of INR 368.45 on October 16, 2025, trading at exorbitant trailing P/E ratios of **765x (2024)** and **1012x (2025)**.
* **Market Dynamics:** Momentum was amplified in the Indian market due to high retail investor participation, less developed short-selling infrastructure, and limited arbitrage capital.

---

## Conclusions
1. **Challenging Traditional Market Efficiency:** Analyzing ARM and Eternal proved that severe mispricing does not merely occur in obscure, illiquid corners of the market, but in highly liquid, heavily scrutinized equities where EMH predicts perfect efficiency.
2. **The Power of Narrative Over Fundamentals:** Working through this empirical study helped me understand that market prices are frequently driven by psychological anchoring, narrative framing, and skewness preference rather than discounted cash flow reality.
3. **Utility of Behavioural Finance:** Behavioural finance is immensely valuable not because it offers a timing formula to predict exact market tops, but because it maps out the predictable psychological mechanisms that sustain irrational price divergences.

---

## Practical Implications
1. **Recognizing Internal Biases:** As an investor, acknowledging that I am subject to the same cognitive biases (such as loss aversion and sunk-cost paralysis) is critical to avoiding emotional decision-making during market manias.
2. **Pre-Committing Safeguards:** To combat behavioural traps like the disposition effect and narrative herding, practitioners must pre-commit to strict risk-management protocols, such as disciplined valuation-based exit rules and predefined stop-losses.
3. **Navigating Market Extremes:** Understanding institutional career risk and herding dynamics allows fund managers to maintain analytical discipline, recognizing that market losses stem less from a lack of information and more from a lack of discipline to act objectively against prevailing market sentiment[cite: 57].
